# Replication-of-Empirical-Asset-Pricing-via-Machine-Learning-Method
In this repository, I replicated the methodologies adopted in the famous Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning (2020) paper by Gu, Kelly and Xiu. I used a range of machine learning methods to construct a portfolio made up by top tech stocks and tried to beat the benchmark return through my portfolio. 

The paper I used is: https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/5/2223/5758276

The data, which includes 94 industry and macroeconomic predictors, such as p/e ratio, are from: https://dachxiu.chicagobooth.edu/. Where Dacheng Xiu (one of the authors of the paper) hosts a free download of the full monthly panel of all 94 characteristics essentially for every US stock. The free data is saved in "Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning".
